    A 900 × 1200 poster with the song's fingerprint, its vital statistics and its three nearest relatives. Download it as a PNG, or copy the plain-text version for a message, a note, or a playlist description.

    A note on the numbers. The five DNA dimensions for catalog records are editorial estimates curated by hand, not lab measurements — they exist to make comparison legible. When you press Deep scan audio, the site downloads a 30-second preview into your browser and measures real loudness, dynamics, brightness, bass weight, stereo width and pulse with the Web Audio API. Songs found through live search receive metadata-based estimates. No audio is uploaded anywhere; analysis happens on your device.
